# Privacy Policy & User Guide

**Last updated:** February 23, 2026

This document outlines the usage guidelines, data processing practices (GDPR compliance), and the End-User License Agreement (EULA) for the **Crossword 3x3** application.

---

## 1. User Instructions

**Crossword 3x3** is an interactive web-based puzzle game.
* **Objective:** Fill the 3x3 grid with the correct letters based on horizontal and vertical hints.
* **Setup:** Before starting, you can configure your username and difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard) in the Settings menu.
* **Scoring:** Points are awarded based on how quickly you solve the puzzle, multiplied by the difficulty coefficient.
* **Persistence:** Your scores and settings are saved locally in your browser.

---

## 2. GDPR & Data Privacy

We value your privacy. This application is designed to be "privacy-first," meaning most data stays on your device.

### 2.1 Data Collection
* **Local Storage:** We store your username, selected difficulty, and game history. This data is stored **only** in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
* **Cookies:** We use a "consent cookie" to remember whether you have accepted or declined our cookie policy.

### 2.2 Third-Party Services
Depending on your choice in the Cookie Popup, this app may use:
* **Analytical Tools:** (e.g., Vercel Analytics or Google Analytics) to collect anonymous data about app usage (e.g., session duration, popular levels).
* You have the right to opt-out by selecting "Essential Only" in the consent banner.

### 2.3 Your Rights
Under GDPR, you have the right to:
1. **Erasure:** You can delete all game data by clearing your browser's Local Storage or cache.
2. **Object:** You can change your cookie preferences at any time.
3. **Anonymity:** You are not required to provide real personal information to play the game.
